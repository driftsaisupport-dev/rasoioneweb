Questions owners ask

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Billing, printing, UPI QR, reports and stock all work offline. Internet is only used to buy the licence or renew online services, send WhatsApp messages and back up to your Google Drive.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Buy RasoiOne once to keep making bills — the licence never ends. If you don't, the app becomes read-only: your old bills, customers and reports still open, and you can still take a backup. Nothing is deleted.

What are online services, and what if I don't renew?

Online services are the table QR menu & QR orders, phone–computer and Google Drive sync, app updates and WhatsApp support. The first year comes with your licence; after that they cost ₹149 / ₹199 / ₹249 a year, only if you want them. If you don't renew, billing, KOT, tables, khata, GST, reports, stock, staff and backups keep working forever.

I paid monthly or yearly before. What now?

Your key keeps working until its paid-until date. When it ends, buy the one-time licence for the same key (in the app, or on the buy page) — your data and devices stay as they are, and you get a year of online services.

Which printers work?

58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers over Bluetooth on Android. On Windows and Mac, any printer installed on the computer.

Can I use one plan on my phone and computer?

The Restaurant + Desktop plan covers two devices that stay in sync over your shop's Wi-Fi. Other plans cover one device.

Is my data safe if my phone is lost?

Turn on backup to your own Google Drive in Settings. On a new phone, install RasoiOne, restore the backup and enter your licence key.

Is it on the Play Store?

The Play Store listing is on its way. Until then, download the APK above — it is the same app.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. You pay once for the licence, and for online services only if you want them. UPI payments go straight from your customer to your bank account.
