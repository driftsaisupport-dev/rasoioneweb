Privacy

Delete your RasoiOne data

For shop owners using the RasoiOne app (developer: RasoiOne)

1. Data on your phone or computer

All bills, menu, customers, table-QR orders and reports are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  1. One customer: in the app open Customers → the customer → Delete customer data (or Export to give them a copy).
  2. Android: phone Settings → Apps → RasoiOne → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  3. Windows: Settings → Apps → RasoiOne → Uninstall. Mac: drag RasoiOne from Applications to the Bin.

If you turned on Google Drive backup, delete the RasoiOne backup folder from your own Google Drive (search “RasoiOne” in Drive). We cannot access it.

2. Table QR menu

The menu lives inside the printed QR code, not on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Throw away the printed QR cards and the menu is gone. Customers' orders stay on their own phones.

3. Licence and referral data

We keep your name, mobile, email, licence key, device id, payment id and (if you are a referrer) your UPI id. You can ask us to delete them. Deleting licence data switches the licence off. Payment and invoice records are kept as long as Indian tax law requires (usually 8 years), and nothing else.

How to request deletion

Email support@rasoione.shop with the subject “Delete my data”, or message us on WhatsApp. Include your shop name, mobile number and (if you have one) your licence key. We confirm by reply within 7 days.
